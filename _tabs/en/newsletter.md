The JustMao weekly is one email a week. It lists every post published on justmao.com the week before, each with its title, link, and summary.

Most of those posts fall into one of these topics:

- **Coding agent skills and workflows.** Skill packs run on real examples, from clarifying requirements to review and merge.
- **Security problems that agents bring in.** What access third-party skills and MCP servers get, and attack chains rebuilt from public evidence.
- **Source-code deep dives into open-source tools.** Reading the source of a specific version to see what a tool actually does.

If nothing new went up last week, no email goes out that week.
